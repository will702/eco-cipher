# Eco-Cipher Product Context

## Register

product

## Product Purpose

Eco-Cipher is a prototype platform that explains how factory waste, emissions, and energy readings become trusted digital evidence. The dashboard connects the physical Edge Verifier device, local ESP32 validation, payload hashing, AI receiver matching, proof records, smart-contract settlement, and CBAM-style reporting.

## Primary Users

- Non-technical pitch audiences who need to understand the proof story quickly.
- Operators inspecting demo sensor states and device behavior.
- Founders, reviewers, and partners evaluating whether the hardware-to-platform flow is credible.

## Core Jobs

- Show what the Edge Verifier senses and why the status becomes verified, warning, or anomaly.
- Make every navigation button feel like a page switch with clear feedback.
- Explain technical ideas in plain language without hiding the real data model.
- Keep the CAD/hardware view connected to the same proof-stage story as the dashboard.

## Tone

Industrial, credible, calm, and demo-ready. The interface should feel like a product control room for a physical prototype, not a generic SaaS landing page.

## Anti-References

- Decorative crypto/neon visuals.
- Dense technical jargon without plain-language explanation.
- Generic card grids where every panel looks equally important.
- UI controls that do not visibly confirm what changed.
